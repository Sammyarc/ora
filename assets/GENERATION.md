# Ora image production

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Room scenes use the generated lamp as a visual reference. These are concept images, not photographs of a manufactured product.

Final assets are WebP, encoded with ffmpeg. Current exports include the lamp (1000×1250, quality 84), room (1600×1280, quality 68), daylight (1600×1000, quality 74), square material detail (800×800, quality 80) and new room-led hero (1800×1012, quality 78). Bedside and dining images retain quality 78. Compression is lossy, not pixel-identical. All seven images were visually reviewed in the rendered page and remain below 100,000 bytes (21,218–80,358 bytes). The lamp cutout retains transparency. Originals remain outside the deployable project. See [HERO-ROOM.md](HERO-ROOM.md) for the new hero prompt and material crop revision.
